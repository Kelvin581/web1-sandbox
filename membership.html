Why SPE

We are the largest organization dedicated to serving managers, engineers, scientists, and professionals in the upstream oil and gas sector. By joining SPE, you become part of a global society of professionals committed to tackling the technical challenges of the oil and gas industry. Membership provides the chance to build a strong network of local and global connections with influential leaders across all disciplines.

Sign-Up Procedure

To become a member of SPE KNUST, please follow these steps:

Step 1: Visit the SPE Website

Go to https://www.spe.org.

Step 2: Navigate to Membership

On the Membership page, click "Join SPE."


Step 3: Choose Student Membership

Scroll down and select "Join Online - Student."

Step 4: Complete Registration

Fill out the required details in the form to finalize your registration.


Finalizing Your Membership

Once you complete the steps above, your membership will be confirmed. You will receive an email with your membership credentials and access to the member portal.

Kindly send our Membership Chair a message via: WhatsApp to be ushered into the KNUST chapter.

Why Renew Your SPE Membership?

Renewing your SPE KNUST membership each year keeps you connected to a global network of petroleum engineers, industry experts, and innovators. It ensures ongoing access to the latest industry knowledge, technical resources, and cutting-edge research. Continued membership also provides valuable opportunities for networking with peers, alumni, and leaders at events, conferences, and workshops, both locally and internationally.
