Our Committees

Programs Committee

Plans and organizes chapter events, seminars, and workshops. Ensures smooth execution and member engagement.

Publicity Committee

Manages chapter communications, branding and social media. Promotes chapter events, initiatives and maintains a strong public presence.

Membership Committee

Handles member registration, renewals, and welfare. Connects new members and supports member retention.

Technology and Innovations Committee

Focuses on integrating technology into chapter operations and exploring relevant innovations. Additionally, this committee handles the chapter's website.

Treasury/Sponsorship Committee

Manages chapter finances, budgeting, and sponsorships. Ensures transparency and accountability.
