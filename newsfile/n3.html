Nhyiraba Paa Kwesi Appiah Wins First Prize at SPE Datathon Across Africa


We are delighted to extend our heartfelt congratulations to Nhyiraba Paa Kwesi Appiah for winning the prestigious first prize in the recently concluded SPE Datathon competition, held in Lagos, Nigeria! This remarkable achievement is a testament to his hard work, dedication, and outstanding skills in data analysis and problem-solving. The SPE Datathon, a challenging competition that brings together brilliant minds from across the continent, tested participants’ ability to work with complex datasets and derive meaningful insights for the energy sector. Nhyiraba’s exceptional performance in the competition highlights not only his individual talent but also his commitment to excellence and innovation in petroleum engineering. As a dedicated member of the SPE KNUST student chapter, Nhyiraba has continually contributed to our growth and success. His accomplishment in the Datathon reflects positively on our chapter and serves as an inspiration to all members. We are proud of his success and grateful for his contributions, which continue to elevate the profile of our chapter. Congratulations once again to Nhyiraba Paa Kwesi Appiah on this well-deserved victory. We look forward to seeing even more great achievements from him in the future!
