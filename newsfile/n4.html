Health, Safety and Environment Seminar


Prioritizing Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) in the energy sector. As part of our goals and mission to bridge the gap between industry and academia, we were pleased to have one of our very own experienced and passionate senior member of the Society of Petroleum Engineers - SPE Ghana Section , Mr. Ato Aidoo came through to visit and have lecture with the student body on HSE. He gave practical, thought-provoking and insightful lessons on the need to prioritize health and safety on various work stations. Mr. Ato Aidoo also made key insights on:
📌 Understanding the stake of HSE
📌 Sustainability and innovation
📌 Collaboration and knowledge sharing
📌 Environmental Stewardship
📌 Regular training drills
📌 Transparent reporting
Special thanks to the Society of Petroleum Engineers - SPE Ghana Section, Ato Aidoo, our faculty advisor, Stephen Adjei, Ph.D. and the Department of Petroleum Engineering, KNUST for giving us the space to execute this educative section.
