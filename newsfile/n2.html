SPE KNUST Members Compete in the Petrobowl at SPE ATCE 2024


We are thrilled to share that two of our talented members, Kelvin Hayford and Joseph Eshun, represented SPE KNUST at the highly competitive SPE ATCE 2024 Petrobowl competition! The event, held as part of the annual SPE Annual Technical Conference and Exhibition (ATCE), brought together some of the brightest minds in petroleum engineering from around the world. The Petrobowl competition, known for its intensity and focus on petroleum industry knowledge, was a true test of skill, speed, and collaboration. Kelvin and Joseph’s journey to the competition was a testament to their dedication and hard work. They prepared rigorously, mastering technical concepts, industry trends, and historical knowledge of the field. Their passion and commitment to excellence stood out, and their participation marked a proud moment for our student chapter. Although the competition was fierce, their performance exemplified the high level of talent that SPE KNUST nurtures. Their participation also served as an inspiration to their peers, proving that with determination and hard work, success is within reach. We couldn’t be prouder of Kelvin and Joseph for their efforts. Their achievements at the SPE ATCE 2024 have not only brought recognition to our chapter but also set an example for future members aiming to compete at such prestigious levels. We extend our heartfelt congratulations to them and look forward to witnessing more great accomplishments in the future. Let’s continue cheering them on as they carry forward the spirit of innovation and passion for the oil and gas industry!
