Safety Symposium


At the SPE x GESA x WAIMM Safety Symposium, we delved into critical safety practices vital for every engineer! Through hands-on fire extinguisher training, recognizing the value of transparency in safety reporting, and unexpected lessons like the critical role of headrests and headlights, the day was packed with valuable insights and engagement.

This symposium underscores the chapter’s dedication to bridging the divide between academia and industry, equipping members with the essential skills and knowledge to excel in any professional environment.
